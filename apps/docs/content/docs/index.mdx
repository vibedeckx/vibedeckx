Vibedeckx runs Claude Code, Codex and experimental fx sessions across your projects and machines, and gives you one place to watch, steer, and review them.

fx sessions require macOS or Linux. See [fx setup and differences](/connect-a-machine#fx-experimental) before choosing it; its capabilities differ from Claude Code and Codex.
